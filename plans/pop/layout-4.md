# Pop layout 4 — section-by-section plan

This is the working checklist for bringing **layout 4** of the Pop template up to its Figma designs,
the way [`../lime/layout-4.md`](../lime/layout-4.md), [`../grunge/layout-4.md`](../grunge/layout-4.md)
and [`../editorial/layout-4.md`](../editorial/layout-4.md) did for the other three. It runs one unit
per session, all three widths together, clearing context between units. Layouts 1 (`s.v0`), 2
(`s.v1`) and 3 (`s.v2`) under `s.pop` are fitted and merged; nothing here should move any of them.
**This pass closes the Pop family**: `HEADER_COUNT.pop` is 4, the setup modal offers four Pop cards,
and after this pass all four are fitted pages. **It also closes the app**: card 4 is the last
placeholder card of any template, so once it ships no card anywhere draws Retro's checker floor
under another template's tokens.

**This plan is Editorial layout 4 again, with Pop layout 3's idiom.** It does not repeat either:
the page order, the wrappers, the procedure, the harness, the digest and the verification are
Editorial's and Lime's layout-4 ones, verbatim, with `theme=3` read as `theme=4`. The gates
(`s.pop`, the widened pair `(s.limeTree || s.pop)`), Titan One at `faceK` 0.98 through `faced` /
